How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag and choose “Order via WhatsApp”. We will confirm availability and the order details with you before you pay.

How much is delivery?

Delivery cost and timing depend on your location. Message us with your area and we will confirm the options before you place the order.

Can I check sizing, colour, or stock?

Yes. Product availability, sizes, and colour options should be confirmed with us on WhatsApp before ordering.

Which payment methods do you accept?

You can request M-Pesa, card, or cash on delivery. We will confirm the available option with your order; do not send payment until availability and the full total have been agreed.
